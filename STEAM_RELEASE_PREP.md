Build 0.178.0 adds sound propagation and stealth. Footsteps, doors, companion movement, weapon clashes, arrows, and battle magic create noises that carry through rooms and across terrain. Walls, partitions, rocks, vegetation, rain, snow, wind, and separate floors change how far sound travels. Press C to crouch: your steps quiet down, enemies must come close to see you, and an unseen target is more vulnerable to your first strike. Enemies follow audible clues and search the last place they heard a sound; battle reports describe the direction and how walls or weather muffled it.
